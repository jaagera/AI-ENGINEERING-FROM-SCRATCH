> Information theory measures surprise. Loss functions are built on it.

* To understand “surprise” as a number, and see that cross-entropy loss, KL divergence, and perplexity are all built from that one idea.

* In information theory, entropy (denoted as H) measures the average amount of uncertainty, surprise, or information contained in a random variable's possible outcomes.
